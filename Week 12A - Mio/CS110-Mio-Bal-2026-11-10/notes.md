video2: - Having the right mindset is essential for game development (I wrote down main ideas then took notes on what I think they mean)
Point 1. "Why is mindset important for game development?" notes:
 '-> Creativity and innovation: the key to game development
 '-> Perseverance: game development is a long process, it is important to stay focused and work towards your goal
 '-> Adaptability: things are always advancing, it is important to keep up
 '-> Collaboration: game development is not a single process, even if you work alone on creating, you are still using assets from someone else
 '-> Customer focus: stay focused on what YOU want to deliver, what audience your making it for, what your players want, that is what leads to success
My conclusion: Staying relevant and focused, while keeping a positive mindset is what leads to a successful game. 

Point 2. "How to develop a growth mindset?" notes:
 '-> Changing the way you think, you have to keep growing throughout the entire process of game development in order to reach your potential
 '-> Embrace challenges: seek out challenges because they are opportunities to grow
 '-> Learn from failure: failure teaches you how to do things differently and learn what you did wrong in order to fix the mistake in the  future
 '-> Focus on effort: have the mindset that your hard work and effort will pay off
 '-> Be open to feedback: feedback is what will give you insight of what others think, giving you room to be criticized so you can improve
 '-> Surround yourself with positive role models: find people who inspire you and you can look up to so you have motivation to keep going
 '-> Keep learning: you will always have to keep learning in life, and in game development, you will never know everything and learning more also relates to having the open mindset 

Point 3. "What are the common obstacles in game development, and how to overcome them?" notes:
 '-> Technical challenges: you will run into issues with sound design or programming, etc., but it is important that you have a team with different technical skills that could help you and to have crisis plans
 '-> Scope creep: it is easy to get carried away in game development, it is important to stay focused
Scope creep=getting carried away from the scope of the game, adding a bunch of different things and features to the original plan.
 '-> Marketing and distribution: a part of creating a game is having an idea of how you will present it to your audience
 '-> Funding: can be an issue because not everyone will have funding, it is important to have a clear business pitch
 '-> Burnout and mental health: game development can be very demanding on your mental health and effort, it is important to take time for yourself as well and to take a lil break if you feel overwhelmed

Point 4. "How to stay motivated and focused during the game development process?" notes:
 '-> Set clear goals list: it will motivate you to achieve goals as you go through the process and help you stay focused on your tasks
 '-> Celebrate small wins: again, celebrating the smaller accomplishments will help you feel happier in general and more motivated
 '-> Take breaks: important that you don't overwhelm yourself and experience burnout, burnout will also just make you dislike the process of game development
 '-> Stay organized: keep things in order and document everything in appropriate folders to find them easier
 '-> Stay passionate: if you are happy and excited to work on a project, you will actually WANT to do it, remember what you are doing it for
 '-> Collaborate and communicate!!!: EXTREMELY IMPORTANT because it can help you enhance everything in your project, it gives you other opinions and ideas and insights which helps you grow and change things and eventually get a better project
